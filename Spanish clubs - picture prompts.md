# Primera Liga: picture prompts for the 20 Spanish clubs

How to use these with ChatGPT:

- **Do one club at a time, and one picture at a time.** Copy one prompt (the text in a grey box), paste it in and let it make the picture. Then do the next prompt.
- Each prompt stands on its own, with the club's name, colours and look written into it, so ChatGPT doesn't need anything from earlier in the chat.
- Starting a **new chat for each club** stops it mixing one club's colours into the next.
- Every club gets four pictures: **the badge**, **the kits**, **the ground from outside** and **the ground inside on a matchday**.
- All the clubs, towns and grounds are made up. They are Spanish in feel but not copies of real clubs, so ask ChatGPT to redo any picture that looks too much like a real club's badge.

The clubs are listed best first, as they are in the game.
