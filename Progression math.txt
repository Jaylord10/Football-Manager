TPS components (0–100)

(A) Space ahead (0–40)
How many metres of free run toward goal?

(B) No blocker (0–25)
Any defender between receiver and goal in a corridor?

(C) Player profile (0–25)
pace + accel + dribble + decisions (scaled)

(D) Lane quality (0–10)
can they attack inside / central? reinforces “not just wide”

Example: LM wide with 20m grass, no blocker, fast player:

Space: 20m → 32/40

No blocker: +25

Profile: +18

Lane quality (wide): +5

✅ TPS ≈ 80
